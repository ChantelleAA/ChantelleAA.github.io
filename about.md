## Who I Am

I'm a PhD researcher at University College Dublin, funded by Innovate for Ireland through the Decarb-AI programme. My research uses AI to help offshore wind turbines in Irish waters withstand a changing climate. I forecast wind extremes with AI weather models and link them to turbine loads and fatigue.

Alongside research, I build applied AI systems and teach machine learning across Africa and Europe.

## Current Work

- **PhD research, UCD:** AI for climate-resilient offshore wind in Ireland. I built an IFORM environmental contour pipeline from ERA5 reanalysis and Marine Institute buoy data, and ran OpenFAST simulations of the IEA 15 MW floating turbine across two 30-year climate periods.
- **AI Delivery Support, Optinergy (Galetech Group):** building AI systems for wind energy operations, including OpsSense, a fault-diagnosis assistant for turbine technicians.
- **Teaching:** tutor at University College Dublin and MMST Academic Tutor at AIMS Ghana.
- **Community:** contributor to GhanaNLP (Ghana Corpus Builder) and member of Masakhane.

## Research Interests
